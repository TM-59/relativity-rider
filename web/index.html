FIELD NOTES

Same distance, less time on board.

At a constant 1 g the rocket reaches the half-way point at 0.95 c. The full flip-and-burn trip takes 3.53 years on Vela's clock and 5.82 years on Oren's. The worldline never leaves the light cone: nothing here goes faster than light.

The same model as the terminal game

Each step advances the ship clock by 0.0044 yr with the exact closed form of hyperbolic motion (units c = 1, years and light-years), in C compiled to WebAssembly. JavaScript only draws and reads inputs. The paintings and the two foxes are illustrations; their markers sit on computed events.
